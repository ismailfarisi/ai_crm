## 4. The problem

## Your quote is in Excel. Your customer is in WhatsApp. Your margin is a guess.

Made-to-order businesses run on five disconnected tools and one person's
memory. A sales rep prices from last year's spreadsheet. Production finds out
about the job when the order lands. Accounts find out when the customer
doesn't pay. Every hand-off loses something — usually money.

Kerf is one system from the first message to the final payment.

---

## 5. The big idea — True Cost™

*(Trademarkable concept, the way Attio owns "Universal Context™".)*

**Eyebrow:** The only CRM with

## True Cost™

Every Kerf quote starts from what the job actually costs to make — not a price
list.

| | |
|---|---|
| **It nests the sheet.** | Kerf works out how many blanks fit on the board you actually buy, grain and gripper margin included. |
| **It counts the step.** | A blank 1mm too wide to fit 4-up costs you 33% more board. Kerf knows. Your spreadsheet doesn't. |
| **It adds the real time.** | Machine hours and labour from your own rates, not a rule of thumb. |
| **It protects the floor.** | Set a minimum margin and a discount cap. Quotes that break them wait for approval. |
| **It remembers the cost.** | Every line keeps the cost it was quoted at, so you can compare quoted against actual later. |

## 6. The journey (five sections, sticky tabs like Attio)

Tabs: **Win the enquiry · Quote it · Make it · Buy & stock · Deliver & get paid**

### 6.1 Win the enquiry

## Every enquiry, wherever it starts.

WhatsApp, email and Telegram land in one shared inbox, matched to the right
customer. Your pipeline shows every open job at a glance — as a table or a
board, filtered and saved the way each person works.

- Contacts, companies and a full activity history
- Board and table views, saved per person
- Custom objects for anything your business tracks — machines, dies,
  certifications
- Roles that decide who sees what, down to "only my own customers"

*Scene: the shared inbox with a WhatsApp enquiry turning into a pipeline card.*

### 6.2 Quote it

## Quote in minutes. Know your margin before you send.

Pick a template, enter the size and quantity, and Kerf builds the cost from
board, print, finishing and machine time. Then it prices to your margin and
lays it out on your branded document.

- Costing formulas built from your own materials and rates
- Margin floors, discount caps and approval rules
- Branded PDF quotes in your layout
- A secure link your customer opens to review and accept online

*Scene: a cost breakdown panel — material, labour, machine, margin — updating
as the quantity changes from 1,000 to 5,000.*

### 6.3 Make it

## The order arrives. The work order is already waiting.

An accepted quote becomes a sales order, and the sales order becomes work
orders with every step laid out. Operators log their hours from the floor —
in the app or with a WhatsApp message.

- Sales orders and staged billing from accepted quotes
- Work orders with steps, scheduling and status
- Time logged against each job, by person and machine

*Scene: WhatsApp — "@Kerf log 3.5h die-cutting on WO-318" → "Logged. WO-318
is 60% complete."*

### 6.4 Buy & stock

## Buy exactly what the job needs.

Kerf knows what each order uses, what's on the shelf and what's already
promised. Raise purchase orders to suppliers in a couple of clicks, and track
bills against what you ordered.

- Stock across multiple locations, with reservations per job
- Reorder points so you don't run out mid-run
- Purchase orders, supplier bills and payables

### 6.5 Deliver & get paid

## From dispatch to the books, without re-typing a thing.

Dispatch the delivery, invoice the customer, record the payment. Every step
posts to a real double-entry ledger, so your P&L, cashflow and VAT are right
the moment the work is done.

- Deliveries, invoices, payments and credit notes
- Double-entry books with a chart of accounts, P&L and cashflow
- VAT and tax rules per country, with multi-currency built in
- Reports your accountant can export

## 14. FAQ

**Is Kerf a CRM or an ERP?**
Both, sized for a growing workshop. It starts where a CRM starts — customers
and enquiries — and keeps going through quoting, production, stock and the
books.

**Do I still need separate accounting software?**
Kerf keeps real double-entry books with invoices, payments, VAT and reports.
Many teams run on Kerf alone; others export to their accountant.

**What industries is it for?**
Any business that makes or finishes to order: packaging, print, signage,
fabrication, joinery and fit-out.

**How do I move from Excel?**
During early access we set you up and bring your customers and products
across for you.

**Is the AI safe to let loose on my data?**
It can draft anything, but it can't change your data or message a customer
without a person approving it. Every action is logged.
