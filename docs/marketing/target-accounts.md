Research date: 2026-10-15. Twenty small-to-mid made-to-order manufacturers in the UAE, weighted to Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah and Dubai industrial areas, to approach as Kerf design partners.

**Read this first.** Most small UAE manufacturers have no real website, so many rows rely on directory listings (Yellow Pages UAE, Make.Works, RAKEZ). I opened every URL below and the details match what is written, but directory data is self-submitted and can be stale. Treat each row as a lead to confirm by phone or WhatsApp, not as verified fact. Sizes are signals, not headcounts, unless stated. Only business-level contact channels are listed; no individuals are named.

## How to approach

General rules: start on WhatsApp or the general office line, ask for the owner or production manager by role, and keep the first message short. Say plainly that Kerf is early-stage, that you are looking for 3-5 founding customers, and that setup is free and guided. Offer founding-customer pricing locked for the first year. Do not claim customers or results you do not have.

**Packaging (corrugated, cartons, thermoformed)**
"Hello, I'm building Kerf, a CRM and quoting tool for made-to-order manufacturers. It prices each box from real board, die, machine and labour cost, and runs from WhatsApp. We are early-stage, so I'm looking for a few founding customers and would set it up with you for free, with locked founding pricing. Could I take 20 minutes to see how you quote a die-cut job today?"

**Print and labels**
"Hello, I'm the founder of Kerf, a new quoting and job-tracking system for printers. It works out price from paper, plates, press time and finishing, and lets customers approve quotes on WhatsApp. It's early, so I'd rather learn from your real jobs than pitch you: free guided setup and founding-customer pricing for the first few shops. Can I watch how one quote goes from enquiry to invoice?"

**Signage and displays**
"Hello, I'm building Kerf, which turns a sign or display enquiry into a costed quote covering sheet, LED, machine time, labour and installation. It is an early product and I'm offering a free guided setup and founding pricing to a handful of shops. If you share one recent job, I'll show you what it would have cost in Kerf. Would you be open to a short call?"

**Metal, laser and CNC**
"Hello, I'm the founder of Kerf, a CRM and quoting tool that prices jobs from material, machine-minute and labour cost, so every cut is counted. We're early-stage and looking for a few founding customers, with free guided setup and locked founding pricing. I'd like to test it on one of your real quotes, with your numbers kept private. Can we talk for 20 minutes this week?"

**Joinery and fit-out**
"Hello, I'm building Kerf, which prices bespoke joinery from board, hardware, shop hours and site time and keeps client approvals in WhatsApp. It is an early product, so I'm offering a free guided setup and founding-customer pricing to a few workshops. I'd value your view on how you cost a project today. Could we speak briefly?"
